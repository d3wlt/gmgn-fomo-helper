How to use

  • Block a token in the tracking feed: Hover over a card, then hold 🚫 beside the token name for one second. Restore it with 🔔 or from the ★ panel.
  • Special watch: Hover over a feed card or wallet row and select ☆. Use ★N in the tracking header to manage watched wallets and blocked tokens.
  • FOMO panel: Use the fomo button on a token page to view holders and narratives. Your own FOMO login is required; the panel explains setup and renews the session locally.
  • Position surge alerts: Open the GMGN positions panel once per chain. Alerts require the cost-return threshold, positive five-minute momentum, and a current balance above zero.

Diagnostics

Keeps up to 500 diagnostic entries for 24 hours on this device. Records request outcomes and feed counts, not tokens, cookies, wallet addresses, names or raw responses. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

Logging off
Data comes from current GMGN cards and optional signed-in FOMO/J7Tracker sessions. No wallet signing or transaction execution is used.
