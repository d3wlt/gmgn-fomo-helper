# GMGN FOMO Helper v0.53.38

## Highlights
- Add market-cap distribution beneath FOMO Trending's chain counts: USD ranges, percentage of the current displayed list, chain-coloured bars and the leading chain in each range, including ties.
- View details expands per-chain coin counts and within-range percentages. Unknown market caps stay separate; statistics follow the displayed snapshot, including while list updates are held.
- Add tracker-matching left-edge stripes for every currently supported Trending chain: Robinhood, Solana, BSC, Ethereum, Base, Monad and Arc. Existing custom chain colours and text labels are retained.
- Preserve the ten-second 🆕 badge and subtle violet highlight for new observed entries. Price/rank updates do not renew the timer; initial, reconnect and Refresh baselines do not mark every coin new.

## Usage
Sign in on FOMO and select FOMO in GMGN's visible Trending panel. Market-cap statistics describe only the coins in that displayed list, not the entire chain or market. Main percentages include Unknown entries in the denominator; expanded percentages describe each chain's share of its range. Ranges include their lower bound and exclude their upper bound; percentages are rounded. All supported chains remain independent of GMGN's chain picker. Expand or collapse View details without additional requests.

## Installation
Download the ZIP and SHA-256 checksum, verify and extract to a permanent folder. Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode and Load unpacked. See GUIDE.md for full instructions.

## Updating
Replace the existing unpacked files, reload the extension, then refresh GMGN and FOMO. Keep the same browser profile and extension identity to preserve settings. GitHub releases do not automatically update unpacked extensions.

## Security and privacy
No additional provider requests, sockets, permissions or persistent ranking data. Statistics reuse validated displayed rows and existing tracker colour preferences. Native trading controls are unchanged; no live trades are needed or performed for verification. Browser fixtures use synthetic token data and do not establish live-provider account coverage.
